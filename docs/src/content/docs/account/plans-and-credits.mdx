MCPAPI charges for what your agents run, not for what they search.

## What costs credits

| | |
| --- | --- |
| A successful tool run | 1 credit |
| Importing your own API | 1 credit per 10 new or changed operations |
| Searches, tool details, failed runs | free |
| Unchanged operations on re-import | free |
| Enabling a public connector | free |

## Plans

The Free plan includes a monthly allowance of credits, every public connector and teams with per-API access. The paid plan adds more credits, your own APIs and MCP servers, reranked search, and extra credits when you need them.

Current prices and allowances are on the [pricing page](https://getmcpapi.com/pricing). Change your plan or buy credits on the dashboard's **Billing** page.
